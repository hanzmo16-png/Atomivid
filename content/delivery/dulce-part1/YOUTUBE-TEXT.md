## Descripción (copiar desde aquí)

For decades, a story has circulated about a secret facility beneath a small town in northern New Mexico. Much of it traces back to one man: Thomas Edwin Castello, who claimed he worked inside.

Part I is a dramatized reconstruction of the account attributed to him. We present his claims as claims, not as established fact.

WHAT CAN BE CHECKED
• Dulce is a real town in Rio Arriba County, New Mexico, and the headquarters of the Jicarilla Apache Nation. Archuleta Mesa lies nearby.
• No official record, document or physical evidence of an underground facility at Dulce has been made public.
• Paul Bennewitz was a real Albuquerque physicist and businessman (Thunder Scientific Corporation, next to Kirtland Air Force Base). Around 1979–80 he became convinced he was intercepting alien signals.

WHAT IS ATTRIBUTED TESTIMONY
• Everything about the inside of the base in this episode comes from an interview attributed to Thomas Castello: the seven levels, the clearances, the grey and reptilian beings, the man called "George", and "Nightmare Hall". The interview circulated in UFO literature from the late 1980s and the 1990s, notably through the writer known as "Branton" (The Dulce Book).
• We have not found records that confirm Castello's identity or employment. That does not prove he never existed. It means his account cannot be checked independently.

WHAT IS HYPOTHESIS
• That parts of the Dulce story grew out of a disinformation effort aimed at Bennewitz. Part II examines this.
• Supported by more than one source: Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI) records released under the Freedom of Information Act show the Air Force was aware of Bennewitz's activities in 1980. Ufologist William Moore said publicly in 1989 (MUFON Symposium) that he had reported on Bennewitz for AFOSI and that false information was passed to him. Former AFOSI agent Richard Doty later described his own role in interviews.
• Resting mainly on one source: the specific tactics, such as staged activity around Dulce, come largely from Doty's own accounts. Greg Bishop's book "Project Beta" (2005) brings these accounts together from interviews.

WHAT IS DRAMATIZED
• All images and scenes in this video are AI-generated reconstructions, not footage or photographs of real places, people or events. Scenes such as Thomas's family life are imagined to illustrate the account. The narration uses a synthetic voice.
