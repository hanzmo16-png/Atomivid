A FEW PRECISIONS
• Ephialtes: Herodotus calls him a Malian, son of Eurydemus. Trachis was the main town of Malis, which is why he is often called "of Trachis".
• "Go tell the Spartans": Herodotus quotes the inscription at the pass. Its attribution to the poet Simonides is traditional; Herodotus names Simonides only as the author of the seer Megistias's epitaph.
• The lambda on Spartan shields: the earliest evidence for it dates to the 420s BC. There is no evidence for it in 480 BC.
• The Thebans: Herodotus says Leonidas kept them back against their will, almost as hostages. Later writers, including Plutarch, disputed his account.

Where sources disagree (the size of the army, why the Spartans stayed, the Thebans' role), the narration says so. Battle scenes are AI-generated reconstructions; maps and figures follow Herodotus and modern estimates. Stock footage: Pexels.
