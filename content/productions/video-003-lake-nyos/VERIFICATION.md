## Visual reconstructions (E1–E6) and the editorial rule

Every generated or reconstructed image is labelled `reconstruction` in its shot contract; only stock footage is
`real_documented`. Uncertain mechanisms are shown as hypotheses, not facts: the landslide (E-trigger) and rain
shots follow the narration "Scientists still cannot say what… any of these could have". No shot depicts victims,
bodies, or death scenes (asserted by `video-003.test.ts`: the forbidden-elements list of every contract includes
"human bodies or victims", and no visual intent matches body/corpse/victim). Villages appear empty; the event is
carried by landscape, atmosphere, diagrams and labelled reconstruction.
