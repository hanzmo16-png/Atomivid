## Selection: Candidate 1 — Lake Nyos / Lake Kivu

Evidence for the choice:
- Factual footing is the strongest of the five: the mechanism (CO2-saturated stratified maar lake, limnic
  eruption) is settled science with primary sources (Kling et al., Science 1987; Kling et al., PNAS 2005;
  Kusakabe et al. 2019 "final step" of degassing), and the numbers we need are documented with known ranges.
- It fits three channel pillars at once: extreme environment, unexplained-then-explained natural
  phenomenon, and a present-day discovery/engineering story; and it is not another ocean film.
- Visual credibility can be high with mostly real or reconstruction-labelled imagery: crater lakes, volcanic
  highlands and night landscapes exist as stock; the eruption itself is a clearly labelled AI reconstruction.
- Cost and complexity fit the acceptance test: one hero AI-motion sequence, the rest animated stills and
  real footage, no character continuity requirement (no recurring human characters are depicted).
- Candidates 2 and 4 carry live scientific disputes on their central spectacle; candidate 5 is saturated;
  candidate 3 is strong but weaker on narrative arc. Candidate 2 remains a good future episode framed
  as "the debate", once the 2025–2026 literature settles.

Residual uncertainty judged NOT material: the exact death toll and CO2 mass are ranges, and the trigger
is unknown. The script states these as ranges and hypotheses, which is documentary-honest, so pre-production
continues to Phase 2.
