# Video #004 — Topic selection (Earthward Chronicles)

Date: 2026-10-01. Topic: **the Battle of Thermopylae (480 BC): Leonidas, the Spartans and their allies against Xerxes' invasion.**

## How the topic was chosen

The topic was set by the operator's production order ("the active production is the Battle of Thermopylae
video"). It was not selected by the candidate-ranking process used for earlier titles, so no candidate table is
reproduced here. The order also instructed that nothing from the halted Lake Nyos production (script, research,
storyboard, manifests, shot assignments, topic decisions) be reused; only the production system and its quality
standards carry over.

## Fit with the channel

Cinematic history is one of the channel's pillars. Thermopylae is evergreen, heavily searched, visually strong
(a coastal pass, a mountain path, two very different armies) and well documented by a single near-contemporary
source (Herodotus, Book 7) plus modern archaeology (Marinatos 1939) and geology (the silted coastline).

## Creative position

- Historical realism, deliberately not the look of the 2006 film: period equipment, real geography, Herodotus'
  own numbers set against modern estimates, the Thespians and helots named, the propaganda claims flagged.
- AI motion is reserved for the battle beats and the night march; everything specific (the monument, the
  epitaph, the arrowheads, museum objects) is real footage or deterministic graphics.
- No blood, wounds or corpses on screen; combat is shown at distance and through equipment.
