### Scenario rules

1. Strategy is an interpretive comparison of historical institutional evidence versus epic characterization.
2. Army scenario: 1,000 each; shield/spear infantry versus mounted missile force; rested, supplied, aware; open dry ground; no prepared ambush, fortress or gods. Equipment and mobility are deliberately unequal. Conditional advantage to mounted force.
3. Confined-terrain alternative: no numerical verdict; objective, routes, waiting and withdrawal change the problem.
4. Duel: both on foot with comparable protection, spear and sidearm; no external/divine intervention. Achilles is a literary favorite, not a measured historical winner.

### Opening edit proposal

0–5 s moving riders in dust; 5–9 s bronze shield line close-up; 9–14 s a rider changes distance; 14–19 s Achilles turns toward the horizon; 19–25 s overhead animated spacing graphic. Only the first, third and fourth shots require new generated action; the diagram is deterministic animation. By 30 seconds state that the meeting never happened. By 60 seconds establish the three contests. Keep the first 90 seconds visually varied without depicting mass melee that cannot be quality-checked.

Later hero animation: one mounted withdrawal at range; one infantry response without collision; one controlled foot-duel setup that stops before impact. Prefer single clear actions to hands, weapons and crowds intersecting in one generated clip.
